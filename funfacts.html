Fun Facts About Netherlands

Discover unique and interesting facts about the Netherlands, from bicycles to tulip fields.

01

More Bicycles Than People

The Netherlands has more bicycles than its population. Cycling is one of the most popular transportation methods.

02

Below Sea Level

About a quarter of the Netherlands lies below sea level, protected by advanced dike systems.

03

Famous Tulip Fields

The Netherlands is known worldwide for its colorful tulip fields that bloom beautifully every spring.

04

Tallest People

Dutch people are considered among the tallest people in the world.

05

Windmills Everywhere

Windmills are iconic symbols of the Netherlands and were traditionally used for water management.

06

Cheese Lover Country

Dutch cheese such as Gouda and Edam are famous around the world.

Why Netherlands is Interesting?

The Netherlands combines modern innovation with strong traditions. Visitors can experience unique culture, beautiful landscapes, and friendly communities.

From bicycles to tulips and canals, the Netherlands offers unforgettable experiences.

“The Netherlands is small in size, but rich in culture and beauty.”
