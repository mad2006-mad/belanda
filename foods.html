Dutch Traditional Foods

Discover delicious traditional foods from the Netherlands, from sweet desserts to famous street snacks.

Stroopwafel

Stroopwafel is one of the most famous Dutch snacks. It consists of two thin waffles filled with caramel syrup.

Bitterballen

Bitterballen is a crispy fried snack filled with meat ragout. It is very popular in cafes and restaurants.

Poffertjes

Poffertjes are small fluffy pancakes usually served with powdered sugar and butter.

Gouda Cheese

Gouda cheese is one of the most famous cheeses in the world and originates from the Netherlands.

Why Dutch Food is Unique?

Dutch cuisine combines simplicity, comfort, and rich traditional flavors. Many foods are influenced by the country’s history in global trade.

From sweet desserts to savory snacks, Dutch food offers a memorable culinary experience.
